Introduction for future users

Deizi.

Kerlenn Pondi’s shared calendar: the group’s dates, everyone’s attendance and all the organisation of each outing, in one place — in each member’s language.

Kerlenn PondiGetting started with Deizi

Why Deizi

One source for every date.

  • 1No more scattered messagesDates, times, places and changes are up to date for everyone.
  • 2Attendance in one clickEveryone answers; those in charge know straight away who will be there.
  • 3Organisation in one placePolls, roadmaps, contracts and quotes follow each date.
  • 4In everyone’s languageFrench, English, Breton, Spanish: each member picks theirs.

Who does what

Five user levels.

Each level sees and does everything the previous one does, with extra rights.

Artists

See the dates of their sections, answer attendance and polls, open the roadmaps.

All members

Managers

Organise their section’s meetings (sectionals, rehearsals), post announcements to it and follow their members’ attendance.

Section or desk leaders

Organisers

Prepare the group’s outings: all dates, polls, roadmaps; create accounts.

Board, coordination

Logisticians

Handle the paperwork of each date: status, contract, fee, expenses, quotes and a report per season.

Treasury, contracts

Administrators

Configure the application: accounts, sections, statuses, site identity, seasons, languages.

Fewer rightsMore rights

Everyone

Logging in.

  • 1Login and passwordGiven by an administrator or an organiser; change them in “My account”.
  • 2Capitals and accents“énora” or “ÉNORA”: the login is recognised either way.
  • 3Computer or phoneThe site adapts to the screen, and installs like an app on the phone.

On your phone: tick “Stay logged in on this device” so you don’t have to log in again (90 days). On a shared computer, leave it unticked.

Everyone

In your language.

  • 1Everyone picks their own“My account”, “Language” section: French, English, Breton, Spanish, or a language added by the association. By default, the site’s language.
  • 2Only the labels changeMenus, buttons, messages and dates. The association’s content (date titles, places, announcements, sections) stays as entered.
  • 3Statuses too“Confirmé” shows as the association named it, whatever the language.

Your calendar (iCal) follows the chosen language too.

Artists

My sections’ calendar.

The next dates of your sections, the latest announcement on top, the filters folded under “Filters”. “Full calendar” also shows past dates; the calendar button subscribes your phone (iCal).

Artists

Answering attendance.

  • 1Present, Absent or Don’t knowIn one click, changeable until the date. Nothing is ticked until you answer.
  • 2“My answers”The “No answer” filter finds the dates still to answer.
  • 3Who is coming?On the date’s page, the answers of each sub-section and the total of each answer.

On the date’s page: the polls to fill in (red dot) and the roadmap of confirmed dates. “New” and a red badge flag dates created or changed.

Everyone

Share and stay up to date.

One unique link, easy to share

Each roadmap and each quote has its own address, sent by organisers and logisticians by message or e-mail: anyone can open and print it without an account (companions, driver, festival organiser…).

Your calendar on your phone

Subscribe once to your personal iCal calendar: the dates arrive in the phone’s calendar and update by themselves (time, place, cancellation).

  1. Calendar button at the top of the calendar
  2. “Subscribe” in the phone’s calendar

Each event shows the polls to fill in and the link to the date.

Deizi on your phone.

Install Deizi on the home screen: it opens with one tap, full screen, like an app.

  • 1iPhone (Safari)Share button, then “Add to Home Screen”.
  • 2Android (Chrome)“Install the app” button, or Chrome’s ⋮ menu.
  • 3Offered automaticallyOn a phone, the calendar offers it in a banner (“Later” hides it for 30 days); everything is also explained on the About page.
  • 4Notifications and bellTurn notifications on from the About page: new date in your sections, a date you opened has changed. Without them, the bell at the top counts them.

The faces of the group, by branch and sub-section: menu, people icon.

DeiziManagers
Managers

My section’s meetings.

The manager creates the internal meetings of their sub-sections, without going through the organisers.

  • 1Sectional“Stage cornemuses”, “Stage Pipe Band”: sections involved, time, place, comment.
  • 2Sub-section rehearsalA date reserved to the members concerned: other sections don’t see it.
  • 3Outside logisticsNo contract or quote: these dates stay out of the logistics follow-up.

Then: everyone answers from the calendar, and the manager follows attendance in the grid.

Managers

Announcements to my section.

A message for all members of their sub-sections, at the top of their home page.

  • 1Title, text, link and photoA change of venue, gear to bring, a reminder before a sectional.
  • 2The right audience, the right timeThe sub-sections in their scope, during the chosen period.
  • 3Home page and notificationThe latest announcement shows above the next dates and goes out as a notification to those who turned them on.

“Announcements” menu (megaphone): “My announcements” to edit or delete. Everyone can hide an announcement once read.

The manager’s form: title, photo, notification. Text, link, period and audience are folded; by default, all sub-sections in their scope.

On a member’s phone, at the top of the home page

Managers

Photos and logins.

The manager helps the members in their scope complete the member directory.

  • 1“Edit” in the member directoryUnder each artist or manager of their sub-sections, and under their own photo.
  • 2Photo and loginA reduced record: change or remove the photo, change the login (the password stays the same).
  • 3Everything is tracedEach change is written to the audit log, with its author.

Remember to tell the member their new login: it is what they will log in with.

Managers

The attendance grid.

Members in rows, dates in columns. A click moves an empty cell to Present, Absent, Don’t know, then back to No answer (keys P, A, N, S); everything is saved automatically.

DeiziOrganisers
Organisers

Polls.

  • 1CreateThe date’s “+ Poll” tab: a question (meal, coach…) and its answers.
  • 2Follow the answersCount and names per answer, members without an answer, vegetarian diet or minors if needed.
  • 3Detailed tableOne row per member, filterable, with totals; printable and exportable as CSV.

For a member: the organiser can enter their answer on their behalf (shown as “entered by …”).

Organisers

The roadmap.

  • 1Filled in with the dateContacts, place, transport, meal, outfit, repertoire, schedule, and documents (access map, organiser’s roadmap…) as a link or PDF.
  • 2Visible to membersAs soon as the date is confirmed; printable as PDF.
  • 3One unique public linkTo copy and send (companions, driver…); can be renewed or turned off.

In iCal: the notes and the roadmap link also reach the phone’s calendar.

Organisers

Accounts and audit log.

Accounts — by branch and sub-section, the lead first; creating artist and manager accounts, sections, scope and login.

Audit log — logins, answers entered, dates changed, profile changes (login, name, title, photo): who, what, when; CSV export. Recorded in English, whatever the author’s language.

Logisticians

Dates, season by season.

  • 1Logistics statusFiner than the calendar’s (quote to send, awaiting contract, to invoice…), it sets the status members see.
  • 2Totals per seasonContracts and fees, latest season first.
  • 3Logistics follow-upOnly dates ticked “Logistics follow-up” appear.
Logisticians

Contract, expenses and fee.

For each date, choose the formula and enter the contract and expenses: the fee is calculated automatically.

Contract−Expenses=Fee
  • 1Formula“Bagad seul”, “Bagad & Cercle”… for the report.
  • 2Admin noteInternal follow-up, shared with the organisers.
  • 3Attached documentsLinks to the contract and other papers, listed under the date’s admin note.
Logisticians

Quotes ready to send.

The organiser quote offers one or more options, each with its lines. A unique public link sends it to the organiser, who prints it without an account.

Draft→Published→AcceptedorRefused

Published = frozen: to change it, withdraw the link and publish again (a new link is created). Extra quotes (transport, accommodation…) are filed by need.

Logisticians

The season report.

  • 1Key figuresDates, contracts, fees, expenses, quotes.
  • 2BreakdownsBy status, by formula (fees included), by section.
  • 3Month by monthWith the season’s totals.

Season: its start day is set in Settings.

Configuring Deizi.

The settings that apply to everyone.

DeiziAdministrators
Administrators

Settings.

Settings by theme — calendar, site settings, members, documents, follow-up and security; in Sections, whether each one shows in the filters and the member directory.

Statuses — names and colours chosen by the association (never translated), logistics statuses and their mapping, with the workflow diagram.

Administrators

Languages and translations.

Correct or delegate — each text under its French version, to correct on screen; or a CSV file to hand to a translator, then import back.

Create a language — a variant (Québec French, Vannetais Breton…) starts from an existing language: only what differs is changed.

Administrators

A member’s record.

Title and lead — the role in the association shown instead of the app role (“Président”); the section lead, first in their sub-section’s lists.

Audit log — a tab of the record: the member’s logins, answers and profile changes.

Administrators

Who can do what?

The rights matrix sums up, action by action, what each role allows. Managers, organisers, logisticians and administrators can open it.

Remember: when in doubt about a right, it is the reference.

Getting started

Four steps this week.

1Log in

With the login and password you received, then choose your language in “My account”.

2Install

Add Deizi to your phone’s home screen.

3Answer

Give your attendance for all the next dates.

4Subscribe

Subscribe your phone with the calendar button (iCal): it stays up to date by itself.
